Fit and fixed quality rules

Clear hair from the forehead and behind both ears. Check TP9 and TP10 contact. Sit still with eyes open and a relaxed jaw. Blink normally. If rail saturation persists, stop and refit.

NB-QC-1: all four channels, 2-second epochs, peak-to-peak ≤200 µV, no |raw| ≥995 µV, SD ≥0.1 µV, no gaps over 20 ms. Ten continuous passing seconds unlock the baseline before every trial. Quiet baseline: 30 seconds.

Primary analysis additionally requires ≥70% accepted epochs in both phases, ≥10 baseline epochs (20 seconds) and ≥5 listening epochs (10 seconds). Short clips can be recorded but cannot enter this primary analysis. No adaptive channel dropping.

Baseline and listening PSDs use a Hann window, 2-second nonoverlapping epochs, constant detrending and mean accepted-epoch power. Theta 4–8, alpha 8–13, beta 13–30 Hz. One result per trial. Epochs are not extra subjects.
