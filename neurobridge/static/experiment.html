Prepare the session
Use headphones, a comfortable volume, and a coded ID. Audio plays on this Mac. Keep this app open throughout the session.
Add one practice passage and your experimental passages. Practice runs first; other clips are randomized. Setup is saved only when you press Save setup. Audio stays in stimuli/audio in your chosen recording folder.
Live conversational understanding
Speak to the subject in the selected language while EEG is recorded. Operator marks Start speaking / Finish speaking, then the subject answers the same two-question format and 1–5 understanding rating. No microphone recording or automatic comprehension prediction. Duplicate a trial to reuse its questionnaire in another language. Avoid repeating the same content for the same subject unless repetition is part of your design.

30-second baseline → 3-second fixation → audio → two questions + 1–5 rating → 5-second rest. The recorder must receive EEG before baseline starts. Up to one hour per session.
