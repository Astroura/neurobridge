NeuroBridge human-voice stimulus collections, 2026-11-19

English Simple v2: 6 complete English recordings, two simple factual multiple-choice questions each.
Arabic Simple v2: 6 complete Arabic recordings, two simple factual multiple-choice questions each. Questions are in English, so participants need to read English. The source locale is ar_eg; these are readings of formal written Arabic, not a claim of conversational Egyptian dialect.
Hindi-English Listening v1: 8 recordings, 4 Hindi / English content pairs. No questions, no understanding rating, no comprehension score. Use NeuroBridge 0.5.1 or newer.

Download a ZIP and keep it zipped. In Session setup choose Import stimulus ZIP, select trials, and add them. Review the response mode and materials, enter a coded subject ID, connect Muse, then start. Packs contain audio, transcripts, manifest, license and review records. Transcripts and keys are operator materials; do not show them before the trial.

Hindi/English entries sharing a content_id are translations of the same source sentence. For a subject, choose only one language per content ID or explicitly counterbalance repetition. The app randomizes non-practice trials; it does not force alternating languages. None of these trials is marked as practice. Choose an independent practice trial before experimental trials if your protocol needs one.

These are short 4-15 second pilot utterances, not matched long comprehension passages. English and Arabic question packs are independent, not translated pairs. Speaker, duration, language and topic can confound comparisons. English questions add an English-reading requirement. Review the complete transcript, answer evidence and independent ASR cross-check in review.json and REVIEW.md. Text/file checks are not a psychometric validation or direct auditory/fluent-speaker certification.

Audio is natural human speech from Google FLEURS, derived from FLORES. Original source sentence ID, split, row, pinned revision, original downloaded WAV hash and modified WAV hash are recorded. Complete utterances are kept. Constant gain moves RMS toward -24 dBFS subject to a -1 dBFS peak limit; resulting RMS can differ. This is not calibrated sound-pressure level or LUFS loudness matching. Adjust the hardware volume before a session and keep it fixed.
