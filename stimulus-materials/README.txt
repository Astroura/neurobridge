IMPORTANT STATUS:
This is the complete CURRENT practice pack, not a validated final study corpus. The three Arabic audio excerpts are consecutive pieces from ONE speaker recording; fixed boundaries may cut sentences and they are not independent passages. Their transcript has not been verified. English source segments may also be incomplete narrative units. Recorded-trial question fields are deliberately blank; do not fabricate answer keys from unreviewed audio. Have a fluent speaker select complete passages and verify transcripts plus two factual questions before collecting study data.

LIVE TRIALS:
Choose one language version of each story per subject, or counterbalance repetition. Hearing a translation of the same story can teach the answers and confound language comparisons. The same two-question format (score 0–2) and 1–5 understanding rating are used for audio and live trials. Keep questions in a language the subject can comfortably read. Operators should read/rehearse the script without showing it to the subject, then press Start speaking immediately before speech and Finish speaking at its end.

No microphone audio is recorded in live mode. Events mark operator button receipt, not acoustic onset. Exact speech, interruptions or deviations should be documented in session notes. The subject should listen silently to reduce movement artifacts. Natural two-way conversation is possible but speaking/movement intervals require separate inspection and should not be pooled with silent listening.

A score of 1/2 means one factual question was answered correctly; it does not demonstrate that exactly half the speech was understood. EEG is not used to predict understanding in this software.

Recordings differ in speaker, genre and acoustics between sources. This pack is appropriate for pipeline practice, not evidence of language-independent comprehension decoding. See LICENSES.txt before redistribution. No participant data are included.
